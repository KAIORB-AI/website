Questions people actually ask

How the pieces fit: the core, the orbits, and the open seat.

How is the KAI247 ecosystem structured?+

Brahmando, the intelligence core, sits at the centre. The first orbit holds the three founding companies. Further out are the network brands, and beyond them an open orbit for the next business to join.

What is an orbit?+

A position in the network. Being in orbit means shared capability reaches your business — hosting, growth, AI, engineering — without your business being absorbed into anyone else's brand.

Who is in the founding orbit?+

Brahmexa LLC (USA), Inducer Solutions Inc (Canada) and JSI Software Solutions (India). Each contributes a different capability to the core.

Can any business enter an orbit?+

Yes. There is an open seat in the outer orbit, and joining is free during the founding period. You keep your name, your look and your ownership.

Does the ecosystem run on shared infrastructure?+

Yes. Hosting, AI agents and growth tooling are provided from the network's own infrastructure across the USA, Canada and India, so a joining business needs no servers of its own.

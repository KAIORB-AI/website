Questions people actually ask

Network-contributed tools — and how they differ from the in-browser utilities.

What is the Instrument Ring?+

Tools contributed by companies in the network and opened to everyone, member or not. The network grows by helping others grow.

What is available right now?+

The LENS workspace for investigating unfamiliar operational data, a free school ERP from JSI Software Solutions, a free ORBIT starter analysis of your hosting from Brahmexa, and the Brahmando marketplace of AI agents and agentic workflows.

How is this different from the free browser utilities?+

The Instrument Ring holds full products hosted by network companies. The utility suite is nine small tools that run entirely inside your browser — nothing you open there is ever uploaded.

Do I have to join KAIORB to use them?+

No. They are open instruments. Joining the network is a separate decision.

What is LENS?+

Brahmexa’s operational data intelligence workspace: you point it at telemetry nobody documented and it profiles every column, marks the ones that are just arithmetic on their neighbours, and proposes reconciliations the data can actually test. The workspace here runs on a synthetic investigation so you can try it without connecting anything.

What a small business actually gets from KaiOrb

Free website and hosting, marketing, local SEO, AI tools and a conversational assistant — and you keep your name and 100% of your business.

9 August 2026

“Free” is a word that has been ruined by people who meant “free until you need it to work”. So it is worth being specific about what KaiOrb gives a business, what it asks in return, and where the edges are.

What arrives

  • A website, with smart hosting on a high-speed CDN. No server for you to run, patch or pay for.
  • Digital marketing and social promotion, through the network's REACH and COMET tooling.
  • Local SEO — the work that decides whether someone searching for your trade in your town finds you.
  • Business tools, including the utility suite anyone can use.
  • NEXUS, a conversational AI assistant that answers from your own content rather than a generic model.

What you keep

Your name, your look, your customers and 100% of your ownership. No equity changes hands, there are no franchise fees, and your brand is never replaced by someone else's. That is the whole difference between a companion ecosystem and an umbrella company.

You do your business. We help everything around it evolve.

The premise is that you already know your craft, your service and your local customers. What you should not have to become is a systems administrator, a marketing department and an AI engineer at the same time.

Why it costs nothing during the founding period

Because the network is forming. Three founding companies — an AI lab in the USA, an agentic-systems firm in Canada, and an education and datacenter business in India — are contributing infrastructure while the orbits fill. A network of four brands is worth less to each of them than a network of forty.

That is the trade, and it is stated plainly rather than hidden in a footer. There is nothing to cancel and nothing to claw back.

Who it fits

Restaurants, landscaping and lawn care, urgent care and dental practices, retail stores and boutiques, construction and trades, event businesses and venues, and professional services like legal, accounting and consulting. Anywhere the work is a craft and the technology around it is a burden.

What it is not

It is not a website builder you log into and operate yourself. It is not a franchise. It is not an agency retainer with a different name. And it will not fix a business whose problem is the business — the network strengthens what is around your work, not the work itself.

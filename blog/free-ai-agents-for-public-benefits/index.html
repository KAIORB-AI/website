Why we built free AI agents for FAFSA, SNAP and VA benefits

Five public agents — student aid, food assistance, housing rights, veterans benefits and naturalization. Zero cost, and a clear line about what they are not.

9 August 2026

The forms that decide whether someone eats, studies, stays housed or becomes a citizen are among the hardest documents ordinary people are ever asked to complete. They are long, conditional, written in the language of the agency rather than the applicant, and getting them wrong is expensive in a way that falls entirely on the person least able to absorb it.

An industry has grown up charging people for help with them. That is the part we object to.

The five that are live

  • FAFSA Helper — federal student aid application guidance.
  • USCIS Companion — N-400 naturalization and work authorization.
  • SNAP Helper — food stamps and Medicaid assistance.
  • Housing Rights Helper — eviction defence and tenant rights.
  • Veterans Navigator — finding VA benefits and local NGO support.

Zero cost, no upsells, nothing to buy at the end. Two more are forming: Medicaid renewal, and workforce rights for wage theft and overtime.

Why a network pays for this

KAI247 is a network of companies that make money building software for other businesses. The impact constellation is what a share of that capability does when it is pointed at people instead of customers.

The network grows by helping others grow. That has to mean something when there is no invoice at the end of it.

The line we will not cross

These agents explain eligibility and walk you through the forms. They are not a lawyer and not a caseworker, and they say so. For a decision carrying legal weight — an eviction hearing, an immigration filing, a benefits appeal — take what the agent produced to a qualified adviser and let them check it.

An AI that overstates its own authority in this domain does more harm than one that does not exist. Being useful and being honest about the limit are the same requirement here.

Propose one

There is an open seat in the constellation for a programme nobody has covered. If you work with people who are stuck on a form that a patient explainer would unstick, tell us which one.

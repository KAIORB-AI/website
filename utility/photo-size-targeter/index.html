How it works. JPEG and WebP are searched by binary-halving the quality between 0.05 and 0.95 to find the highest quality that still fits. If even the lowest quality is too large, the image is scaled down in steps and the search runs again. PNG is lossless, so only resolution is reduced. Every attempt happens on a canvas in this tab.

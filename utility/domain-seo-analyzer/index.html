Frequently Asked Questions

What DNS records are inspected?

The tool resolves A (IPv4 host addresses), MX (mail servers), NS (authoritative nameservers), and TXT (SPF/verification records) using Google & Cloudflare DNS over HTTPS (DoH).

What on-page SEO signals are scored?

We evaluate title length, meta description quality, H1 hierarchy, HTTPS encryption, viewport responsiveness, canonical URLs, robots.txt, sitemaps, and Open Graph social tags.

Video to Audio

Take the audio out of a video

Drop in an MP4 and get a WAV back. Useful for transcription, podcast edits, and anything that wants sound without pictures.

Your video never leaves this tab. It is decoded by your own browser's media engine and re-encoded here. There is no upload and no server round trip — you can disconnect from the network and this page still works. That also means nobody at KAI247 can see the file, which matters if it is a client recording.

How it works. The file is handed to your browser's own audio decoder, which reads the audio track out of the container and ignores the video frames entirely. The result is resampled and mixed to the shape you asked for, then written out as a RIFF/PCM WAV. Nothing is transcoded on a server, so quality is limited only by what was in the original recording.

What it cannot do. WAV is uncompressed — expect roughly 10 MB per minute at CD quality in stereo, or about 1.8 MB per minute at 16 kHz mono. The whole track is held in memory while it is converted, so very long recordings can exhaust a tab; if that happens, the page says so rather than hanging. A handful of exotic codecs are not decodable by every browser, and a video with no audio track cannot produce a file — both are reported plainly instead of downloading an empty WAV. There is no MP3 output here: MP3 encoding needs a licensed encoder this page deliberately does not ship.

Questions people actually ask

Nine instruments, all free, all running entirely in your browser. No account, no upload, no watermark and no paid tier holding back the setting you actually need.

Are these tools really free?+

Yes. No account, no email, no trial, no watermark and no export limit. They are published by KAI247 as part of what the network gives away.

Why do they run in the browser instead of on a server?+

Because most of these jobs involve a file you would rather not hand over: a signed contract, a passport scan, a client recording, an internal address plan, your own revenue figures. If the file never leaves the tab, there is nothing to trust us with.

Do they work offline?+

Once a page has loaded, yes. You can disconnect and keep working, which is the simplest demonstration that nothing is being uploaded.

What is the catch?+

There is not one for you. KAI247 is a network of companies that build software, and these tools are a plain demonstration of how we work. If you never contact us, the tools still work.

Can I suggest a tool?+

Yes — through the contact page. The suite grows from what people actually ask for.

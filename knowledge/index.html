The network

What is KAI247?

A companion ecosystem — a shared intelligence, technology and growth network that businesses join while keeping their own identity. Not an umbrella company, not a franchise.

Intelligence sits at the center, capability surrounds it, real businesses are already in orbit, and one seat is always open. See how it works →

Who founded KAI247?

Three companies on three continents: Brahmexa LLC (USA), Inducer Solutions LLC (Canada) and JSI Software Solutions Pvt Ltd (India) — delivering as one network rather than three vendors. Meet the network →

What is Brahmando?

The intelligence core — the Brahmexa platform for AI agents, MCP servers and agentic workflows, available to Brahmexa customers and community partners. About the core →

Which businesses are already in orbit?

PrimoVive (USA, single-use breathalyzers), Isha Skin Care & Hair Clinic (India, dermatology), Global & Funsize Productions (USA, live entertainment production) and Easy Q2C (Australia).

Each keeps its own name, identity and ownership.

Joining

What does it cost to join?

Free during the founding period. The network provides hosting, infrastructure and shared capability while the orbit forms.

Paid plans arrive later and founding brands hear first. Terms are shaped brand by brand. Apply as a brand →

Does my business lose its identity?

No. Nothing is surrendered. The network adds capability around your business — web, hosting, SEO, marketing, AI, automation, engineering, utilities — it does not absorb it.

Can my company join as a provider instead?

Yes. Providers contribute capability — an AI datacenter, AI offerings for small businesses, cloud, engineering, growth or education.

Share your offering as a URL, a spreadsheet or any other form, and the network gets in touch. Offer your capability →

Who should offer a capability, and where it ends up.

Who should offer a capability?+

Providers of AI datacenter and GPU compute, AI offerings for small businesses, cloud and hosting, software engineering, growth and marketing, or education technology.

What happens to my offering?+

It powers a constellation — the cluster of capabilities that network brands draw on. Instead of selling to one business at a time, your capability reaches the brands already in orbit.

Does it cost anything to offer a capability?+

No. The founding period is free in both directions: brands pay nothing to join and providers pay nothing to be listed into a constellation.

What capabilities does KaiOrb offer?+

Eight constellations: AI and intelligence, software engineering, web and commerce, growth and marketing, cloud and infrastructure, education technology, events and engagement, and data and transformation.

What is a constellation?+

A cluster of related capabilities that work together. Selecting one opens the agentic workflows inside it, so you can see the stages a piece of work actually moves through.

Are these delivered by people or by automation?+

Both. Agentic workflows carry the repeatable stages — intake, enrichment, checks, publishing — and the founding companies stand behind the parts that need judgement.

Do I need technical staff to use any of this?+

No. Hosting, growth, AI and digital capabilities arrive without technical complexity. That is the point of the network: you keep doing your craft.

Can a capability be added to a constellation?+

Yes. Providers can offer AI compute, cloud, software engineering, growth or education capability into a constellation, where it reaches network brands worldwide.
